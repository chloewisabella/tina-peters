Mesa County, Colorado  ·  A Three-Part Investigation

Who Is
Tina Peters?

She was a 63-year-old political newcomer who ran on reopening DMV offices. She became the first election official in American history convicted of crimes tied to overturning a presidential election. In June 2026 she will be released on parole. This project is an attempt to understand how it happened.
